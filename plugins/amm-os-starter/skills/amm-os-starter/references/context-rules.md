## Confidentiality

Never write in this folder, nor in a conversation with an AI:

- a national ID (CIN) or passport number;
- bank details (RIB), card number;
- passwords, access keys, codes;
- personal data about clients: name of a private individual, phone, email, address.

To talk about a client case, use an anonymised case ("a transport SMB in Casablanca"). Private files and folders are listed in `.claudeignore`.

## Law 09-08: reminder

Moroccan law 09-08 protects individuals with regard to the processing of their personal data. It is overseen by the CNDP (the national commission for the protection of personal data). In practice: collect only the data you need, do not hand it to a tool without necessity, and know who it serves.

This is a simple reminder, not legal advice. For a specific situation (client file, prospecting, declaration to the CNDP), ask a lawyer or check the CNDP website.
